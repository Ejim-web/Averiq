What is Averiq?

Averiq is a platform that lets you earn AVQ rewards through daily check‑ins, mining sessions, and referrals. It is being developed as a future cryptocurrency token on Solana.

What can you do on Averiq?

  • Daily Check‑In — Earn AVQ every day.
  • Mining Sessions — Start a session and earn rewards.
  • Referrals — Invite friends and earn bonus AVQ.
  • Wallet — Manage your off‑chain and future on‑chain AVQ balances.

How Averiq Works

Building a useful digital ecosystem.

1. Create an Account

Sign up with your username and email. No personal information is required beyond basic details.

2. Earn Off-Chain AVQ Rewards

Participate in daily check‑ins, mining sessions, and referrals to earn AVQ rewards. All rewards are recorded in the Averiq reward ledger.

Important: AVQ is currently an off‑chain reward. On‑chain token will be deployed in the future.

3. Claim Your Rewards

When your rewards become eligible, go to the Rewards page and tap CLAIM REWARDS to add them to your off‑chain balance.

4. Connect Your Solana Wallet

For future on‑chain AVQ, connect your Solana wallet. Currently only Devnet is supported for testing.

5. Future On-Chain AVQ

When the AVQ SPL token is deployed, you will be able to convert off‑chain rewards to on‑chain AVQ.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions.

What is Averiq?

Averiq is a digital rewards platform that allows users to earn AVQ through daily check‑ins, mining sessions, and referrals.

What is AVQ?

AVQ is the native token of the Averiq ecosystem. Currently, it is an off‑chain reward balance. Future plans include deploying AVQ as an SPL token on Solana.

How does AVQ Mining work?

Mining sessions are time‑based activities where you earn AVQ for participating. This is not Proof‑of‑Work mining.

Is Averiq currently a blockchain?

No, Averiq is currently a centralized reward ledger. A token on Solana is planned for the future.

Can I withdraw AVQ?

Currently, withdrawals are not available. On‑chain withdrawals will be enabled after the AVQ SPL token is deployed on Solana.

Does AVQ have a market price?

No, AVQ is not yet a traded cryptocurrency. It has no market price.

How are community rewards calculated?

A portion of platform revenue is allocated to a Community Reward Pool. Users earn activity points, and the pool is distributed proportionally at the end of each reward period.

How can I contact Averiq?

You can reach us via the Contact page.
