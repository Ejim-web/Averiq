What is Averiq?

Averiq is a platform that lets you earn AVQ rewards through daily check‑ins, mining sessions, and referrals. It is being developed as a future cryptocurrency token on Solana.

Currently, AVQ is an internal reward ledger. On‑chain token and withdrawals will become available after the AVQ token launch on Solana.

What can you do on Averiq?

  • Daily Check‑In — Earn AVQ every day and build your streak.
  • Mining Sessions — Start a session and earn rewards based on time.
  • Referrals — Invite friends and earn bonus AVQ.
  • Wallet — Manage your off‑chain and future on‑chain AVQ balances.
  • Future Solana Token — AVQ will become a real SPL token on Solana.
  • Community Rewards — A portion of platform revenue is allocated to eligible active users.

About Averiq

Our mission: to create a fair and transparent digital rewards ecosystem.

Our Vision

Averiq is designed to be a community‑driven platform where users are rewarded for their participation. We are building a system that will eventually transition to a token on the Solana blockchain, giving users true ownership of their AVQ tokens.

Current Phase

Currently, Averiq operates as a rewards ledger. Users earn AVQ through daily check‑ins, mining sessions, and referrals. All rewards are recorded on the server and are eligible for future conversion to on‑chain SPL tokens.

Important: AVQ is not yet a blockchain token. On‑chain token and withdrawals will be enabled after the AVQ SPL token is deployed on Solana.

Future Plans

  • AVQ SPL Token — Deploy an SPL token on Solana.
  • Real Wallets — Users will hold AVQ in their Solana wallets.
  • On‑Chain Transactions — Send and receive AVQ on Solana.
  • Decentralized Governance — Community voting on key decisions.

How Averiq Works

Simple steps to start earning AVQ.

1. Create an Account

Sign up with your email or username. No personal information is required beyond basic details.

2. Start an AVQ Mining Session

Navigate to the Mining page and tap START MINING. Your session will run for a configured duration (e.g., 1 hour). After the session completes, your reward becomes eligible to claim.

Note: This is not Proof‑of‑Work blockchain mining. It is a participation‑based reward system. Future versions may incorporate real blockchain consensus.

3. Daily Check‑In

Visit the app daily and tap CHECK IN to earn a reward. Your streak increases with consecutive days, unlocking higher rewards.

4. Claim Eligible Rewards

When your mining session completes or you check in, rewards become eligible. Go to the Rewards page and tap CLAIM REWARDS to add them to your off‑chain balance.

5. Invite Friends

Use your unique referral code to invite others. When they join and participate, you earn referral bonuses.

6. Manage Your Wallet

Your off‑chain AVQ balance is displayed on the Wallet page. When the SPL token is live, you will be able to withdraw to your Solana wallet.

How Averiq Rewards Work

Transparent community rewards funded by platform revenue.

Community Reward Pool

Averiq may allocate a portion of eligible platform revenue (e.g., advertising income) to a Community Reward Pool. This pool is then distributed among active, eligible users based on their participation and activity points.

Important: Rewards are not guaranteed. They depend on platform revenue and the applicable eligibility rules. Reward amounts are estimated and finalized at the end of each reward period.

How Are Rewards Calculated?

Users earn activity points for:

  • Daily Check‑Ins — Points for each check‑in.
  • Mining Sessions — Points for completing sessions.
  • Referrals — Points for eligible referrals.
  • Other qualifying activity as determined by Averiq.

At the end of each reward period (e.g., monthly), the Community Reward Pool is divided proportionally among all eligible users based on their total activity points.

Reward Periods & Finalization

Rewards are calculated at the end of each period. The final amount is determined after:

  1. Verifying platform revenue for the period.
  2. Applying the configured community allocation percentage.
  3. Calculating each user's share based on activity points.
  4. Creating reward records.

Users will see estimated rewards during the period, and final rewards after the period ends.

No Guaranteed Income

Rewards are not guaranteed income or profit. They are a share of actual platform revenue, which may vary. Users should not rely on rewards as a source of income.

AdSense Compliance

We do not incentivize users to click ads, generate fake traffic, or engage in artificial activity. Advertising revenue is generated naturally through legitimate ad placements. Reward eligibility is based on genuine platform participation, not ad interaction.
