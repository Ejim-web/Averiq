What is Averiq?

Averiq is a platform that lets you earn AVQ rewards through daily check‑ins, mining sessions, and referrals. It is being developed as a future cryptocurrency token on Solana.

Currently, AVQ is an internal reward ledger. On‑chain token and withdrawals will become available after the AVQ token launch on Solana.

What can you do on Averiq?

  • Daily Check‑In — Earn AVQ every day and build your streak.
  • Mining Sessions — Start a session and earn rewards based on time.
  • Referrals — Invite friends and earn bonus AVQ.
  • Wallet — Manage your off‑chain and future on‑chain AVQ balances.
  • Future Solana Token — AVQ will become a real SPL token on Solana.

About Averiq

Our mission: to create a fair and transparent digital rewards ecosystem.

Our Vision

Averiq is designed to be a community‑driven platform where users are rewarded for their participation. We are building a system that will eventually transition to a token on the Solana blockchain, giving users true ownership of their AVQ tokens.

Current Phase

Currently, Averiq operates as a rewards ledger. Users earn AVQ through daily check‑ins, mining sessions, and referrals. All rewards are recorded on the server and are eligible for future conversion to on‑chain SPL tokens.

Important: AVQ is not yet a blockchain token. On‑chain token and withdrawals will be enabled after the AVQ SPL token is deployed on Solana.

How Averiq Works

Simple steps to start earning AVQ.

1. Create an Account

Sign up with your email or username.

2. Start an AVQ Mining Session

Navigate to the Mining page and tap START MINING. Your session will run for a configured duration. After completion, your reward becomes eligible to claim.

Note: This is not Proof‑of‑Work blockchain mining. It is a participation‑based reward system.

3. Daily Check‑In

Visit the app daily and tap CHECK IN to earn a reward. Your streak increases with consecutive days.

4. Claim Eligible Rewards

Go to the Rewards page and tap CLAIM REWARDS to add them to your off‑chain balance.

5. Invite Friends

Use your unique referral code to invite others.

6. Manage Your Wallet

Your off‑chain AVQ balance is displayed on the Wallet page. When the SPL token is live, you will be able to withdraw to your Solana wallet.

How Averiq Rewards Work

Transparent community rewards funded by platform revenue.

Community Reward Pool

Averiq may allocate a portion of eligible platform revenue to a Community Reward Pool. This pool is then distributed among active, eligible users based on their participation and activity points.

Important: Rewards are not guaranteed. They depend on platform revenue and the applicable eligibility rules.

How Are Rewards Calculated?

Users earn activity points for Daily Check‑Ins, Mining Sessions, and Referrals. At the end of each reward period, the pool is divided proportionally.

AdSense Compliance

We do not incentivize users to click ads. Advertising revenue is generated naturally through legitimate ad placements.
