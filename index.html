What is Averiq?

Averiq is a platform that lets you earn AVQ rewards through daily check‑ins, mining sessions, and referrals. It is being developed as a future cryptocurrency ecosystem, with plans for a dedicated blockchain and on‑chain token.

Currently, AVQ is an internal reward ledger. On‑chain withdrawals will become available when the AVQ blockchain infrastructure is launched.

What can you do on Averiq?

  • Daily Check‑In — Earn AVQ every day and build your streak.
  • Mining Sessions — Start a session and earn rewards based on time.
  • Referrals — Invite friends and earn bonus AVQ.
  • Wallet — Manage your AVQ balance and transactions.
  • Future Blockchain — AVQ will become a real cryptocurrency with on‑chain transfers.

About Averiq

Our mission: to create a fair and transparent digital rewards ecosystem.

Our Vision

Averiq is designed to be a community‑driven platform where users are rewarded for their participation. We are building a system that will eventually transition to a fully decentralized blockchain network, giving users true ownership of their AVQ tokens.

Current Phase

Currently, Averiq operates as a rewards ledger. Users earn AVQ through daily check‑ins, mining sessions, and referrals. All rewards are recorded on the server and are eligible for future conversion to on‑chain tokens.

Important: AVQ is not yet a blockchain cryptocurrency. On‑chain withdrawals will be enabled after the AVQ blockchain is launched.

Future Plans

  • AVQ Blockchain — A dedicated network with consensus mechanisms (PoW, PoS, or PoA).
  • Real Wallets — Users will hold AVQ in their own wallets.
  • On‑Chain Transactions — Send and receive AVQ on the blockchain.
  • Decentralized Governance — Community voting on key decisions.

How Averiq Works

Simple steps to start earning AVQ.

1. Create an Account

Sign up with your email or username. No personal information is required beyond basic details.

2. Start an AVQ Mining Session

Navigate to the Mining page and tap START MINING. Your session will run for a configured duration (e.g., 1 hour). After the session completes, your reward becomes eligible to claim.

Note: This is not Proof‑of‑Work blockchain mining. It is a participation‑based reward system. Future versions may incorporate real blockchain consensus.

3. Daily Check‑In

Visit the app daily and tap CHECK IN to earn a reward. Your streak increases with consecutive days, unlocking higher rewards.

4. Claim Eligible Rewards

When your mining session completes or you check in, rewards become eligible. Go to the Rewards page and tap CLAIM REWARDS to add them to your balance.

5. Invite Friends

Use your unique referral code to invite others. When they join and participate, you earn referral bonuses.

6. Manage Your Wallet

Your AVQ balance is displayed on the Wallet page. You can view transaction history and, in the future, withdraw AVQ to external wallets once the blockchain is live.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Averiq.

What is Averiq?

Averiq is a digital rewards platform that allows users to earn AVQ through daily check‑ins, mining sessions, and referrals. It is being developed as a future cryptocurrency ecosystem.

What is AVQ?

AVQ is the native token of the Averiq ecosystem. Currently, it is an internal reward balance. Future plans include launching a dedicated blockchain and on‑chain token.

How does AVQ Mining work?

Mining sessions are time‑based activities where you earn AVQ for participating. This is not Proof‑of‑Work mining; it is a participation reward. Future versions may include real blockchain mining.

Is Averiq currently a blockchain?

No, Averiq is currently a centralized reward ledger. A blockchain is planned for the future.

Is the current Mining Session Proof‑of‑Work?

No, the current mining session is a participation reward. It does not perform any cryptographic work. It is clearly labeled as "AVQ Mining Session" to avoid confusion.

How do I earn rewards?

Rewards are earned by completing daily check‑ins, running mining sessions, and referring friends. All rewards become eligible for claiming after verification.

How does Daily Check‑In work?

Each day you can check in once. Your streak increases with consecutive check‑ins, and the reward amount grows based on your streak.

How do I claim rewards?

Go to the Rewards page, view your eligible rewards, and tap CLAIM REWARDS. The system will verify and credit your balance.

How do referrals work?

Each user has a unique referral code. Share it with friends; when they sign up and participate, you earn referral bonuses.

Can I withdraw AVQ?

Currently, withdrawals are not available. On‑chain withdrawals will be enabled after the AVQ blockchain is launched. This is a planned feature.

When will on‑chain withdrawals be available?

We are actively developing the AVQ blockchain. An estimated timeline will be announced as we progress.

Does AVQ have a market price?

No, AVQ is not yet a traded cryptocurrency. It has no market price, and we do not speculate on future value.

How can I contact Averiq?
